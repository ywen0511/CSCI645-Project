# v2 protocol (frozen at tag `experiment-v2`)

Machine-readable: [`configs/v2/frozen/protocol.json`](../../configs/v2/frozen/protocol.json) (SHA-256 in
`configs/v2/frozen/PROTOCOL_SHA256`), generated by `scripts/make_v2_protocol.py` and resolved by
`scripts/freeze_v2.py`. Suites and hashes: `configs/v2/suites/SUITES.json`. Decisions and evidence:
[DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md). v2 is designed **after** v1 and the v2 development diagnostics; fresh held-out
cases address case reuse, not the independence of the hypothesis from v1 findings.

## What stays as in v1

Single rigid bar, two Franka Panda arms with parallel-jaw grippers, fixed orientations, Isaac Lab v3.0.0 /
Isaac Sim 6.2.0.0 / PhysX (`isaacsim_physx`), 120 Hz physics, 20 Hz control, the same nominal schedule,
observation vector (46 dims, phase channels 42:46), 3-D receiver position residual `0.02 * tanh(a)` per axis,
reward terms and weights, PPO (RSL-RL 5.5.1, 1,024 environments, 32-step rollouts, same hyperparameters),
success definition (two continuous seconds of receiver-only holding, giver open, no giver or table support)
and the shared evaluator.

## What changes

| Item | v1 | v2 |
|---|---|---|
| Training distribution (curriculum by transition fraction) | 0 -> 5 mm -> 1 cm / 0.15 s | 0 (0-10%) -> 1 cm / 0.15 s (10-30%) -> 1.5 cm / 0.225 s (30-50%) -> **2 cm / 0.30 s** (50-100%) |
| Geometry in combined / combined_no_phase | 1 cm alignment bound | **1.5 cm** alignment bound (stronger default comparator) |
| Actor initialization | RSL-RL default (non-zero mean) | **zero-mean output layer** (pilot P2): step-0 residual exactly 0; exploration std 0.5 unchanged |
| Checkpoint candidates | step 0 + first at/after 2, 4, 6, 8, 10M | first at/after 2, 4, 6, 8, 10M; **step 0 excluded** (kept as untrained control) |
| Selection tie-breaks | drop rate, earliest step | collision-episode rate, mean bounded residual squared norm, **latest** step |
| Validation | 100 cases from the training distribution | 400 cases in 5 strata (below) |
| Final suite | 9 conditions x 200 | 9 v1 condition structures with fresh draws + 3 extrapolation conditions, 200 each = 2,400 |
| Controls | nominal, geometry | + constant offsets, geometry component ablations, alignment-bound sweep, untrained policies |
| Records | `episode_return` without terminal step (erratum E2) | full return + terminal-step reward |

## Training

Methods residual, combined, combined_no_phase (combined_no_phase zeroes only the four explicit phase
channels; raw contact forces, widths and the clock remain observable). Seeds 11, 22, 33. 10M requested
transitions per run, realized 10,027,008 (306 rollouts x 32,768). Transitions count every vectorized step
including reset settling; scored (control-active) transitions are logged per iteration. Checkpoints every
1M transitions plus step 0, final and a resumable `latest.pt`.

## Selection

Validation suite `configs/v2/suites/validation.jsonl` (seed 6453027, 400 cases):

| stratum | cases | position | release offset | relation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| clean | 20 | 0 | 0 | in training (deterministic repeats) |
| in_range | 80 | +-1 cm uniform | +-0.15 s uniform | in training |
| train_full | 100 | +-2 cm uniform | +-0.30 s uniform | in training (final stage) |
| train_edge | 100 | every axis 1.5-2 cm, random sign | +-0.30 s uniform | in training (edge) |
| harder | 100 | +-2.5 cm uniform | +-0.375 s uniform | beyond training, inside the extrapolation range |

On the development-seed proxy of these strata, 1.5 cm geometry succeeds on 394/400 (only the harder
stratum is below 100%), so validation is near-saturated for the stronger comparator. The rule may then be
decided by its tie-breaks; this is disclosed and excluding step 0 does not fix it.

Rule (`csci645_handover.selection.select_v2`): highest validation success; then lowest collision-episode
rate; then lowest mean bounded residual squared norm over scored steps; then the latest transition count.
Both the selected and the final checkpoint of every run are evaluated (separately declared analyses).

## Final evaluation

Suite `configs/v2/suites/final.jsonl` (seed 6453026), 200 cases per condition:

| condition | v1 structure | position | release offset | relation to v2 training |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| clean | clean | 0 | 0 | in training (deterministic repeats) |
| position_1cm | position_in | +-1 cm | 0 | in training |
| position_2cm | position_stress | +-2 cm | 0 | in training (full final-stage range) |
| timing_early_015 / timing_late_015 | timing_*_in | 0 | -/+0.15 s | in training |
| joint_early_1cm_015 / joint_late_1cm_015 | joint_*_in | +-1 cm | -/+0.15 s | in training |
| joint_early_2cm_030 / joint_late_2cm_030 | joint_*_stress | +-2 cm | -/+0.30 s | in training (corner) |
| position_3cm | — | +-3 cm | 0 | extrapolation |
| joint_early_3cm_045 / joint_late_3cm_045 | — | +-3 cm | -/+0.45 s | extrapolation |

The extrapolation conditions were admitted by a development feasibility check (all resets valid; 2 cm
geometry 90-100%). Groups: inner (6 conditions), outer (3), extrapolation (3).

Controllers (all on identical cases, deterministic inference, frozen normalization): nominal; geometry 1 cm
(historical); geometry 1.5 cm (stronger comparator); geometry 1.5 cm + constant (+10 mm x; primary constant
control); geometry 1 cm + constant (+7 mm; diagnostic); nominal + constant (+9 mm); the eight A/H/G
component combinations at 1 cm (as in development) and, exploratory, at 1.5 cm; alignment bounds 0.5, 1.0,
1.5, 2.0 cm; for each of the nine runs the step-0, selected and final checkpoints. Identical deterministic
configurations are evaluated once and reused with an explicit index (`final_manifest.json`).

## Analysis plan

Primary (selected checkpoints): combined - geometry 1.5 cm; combined - (geometry 1.5 cm + constant);
residual - nominal; residual - (nominal + constant); combined - combined_no_phase. Declared secondary: the
same with final checkpoints. Exploratory: component ablations and factorial effects, bound sweep, untrained
vs zero residual, 1 cm settings, per-condition differences. Metric: success over valid resets (also
scheduled-case success, drop, collision-episode, timeout and release-timeout rates). Uncertainty: paired
two-level bootstrap (10,000 replicates; training seeds resampled within method, shared case IDs
resampled jointly), 95% percentile intervals; Holm-adjusted bootstrap sign p-values for the five primary
comparisons. Reported per seed, per condition, per group and as equal-condition means. Three training
seeds limit seed-level inference.
